Bit identity with WRF

In v1.4.0, CPU CI passed 531 checks against WRF at tolerance 0, CUDA FP64 passed 423 checks against WRF real*8 at tolerance 0, and CUDA FP32 identity passed 44 checks against WRF real*4 at tolerance 0.

Verification method and coverage

WRF inputs and outputs

CPPWRF ports the dynamical core, physics, static nesting, real.exe and ideal.exe. It reads WRF’s wrfinput and wrfbdy and writes wrfout and wrfrst.
